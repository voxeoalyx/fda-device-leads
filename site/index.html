How a sales rep uses it

  1. Filter by state or product code to match your territory or category.
  2. New 510(k) clearances are companies about to launch a product, so they may need distribution, components, testing or marketing.
  3. New listings are new or newly registered manufacturers and importers.
  4. Recalls flag companies that may need quality or regulatory help.
  5. Open the source link and verify before reaching out.
